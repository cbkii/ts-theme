au-state-rings.tsv.gz: derived from Australian Bureau of Statistics,
ASGS Edition 4 (2026), State and Territory digital boundaries.
https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2026/STE/MapServer/0
Copyright Commonwealth of Australia. Licence: CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: WGS84 export, geometry simplification/rounding, field extraction and gzip.
Not an official ABS product or a legal boundary. Reference matching is inferred,
not authoritative tuned-frequency or signal-coverage evidence.
